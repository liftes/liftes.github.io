RESEARCH PROFILE

From soft-matter theory to complex networks.

I am a theoretical physicist working across soft condensed matter and complex systems. My research centres on analytical derivation and model building: self-consistent field theories for sliding-topological and topology-transformable polymers, diffusion theory in hyperuniform media, combinatorial optimization for complex free-energy landscapes, and phenomenological descriptions of exciton transport in organic solar cells.
